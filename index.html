How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. We quote plain, transparent pricing after the free lead-leak audit, once we see what is already working and what actually needs to be fixed.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Ongoing management is month-to-month. You stay because it produces calls and customers, not because of a lock-in clause.

How long until it is live?

Most website, SEO, and AI voice builds go live in 3 to 4 weeks after the audit call, depending on scope.

Does the AI phone agent sound robotic?

It speaks in a natural voice, answers specific questions about your business, hours, and services, and transfers or texts your team immediately when a person is needed. Request a free audit and we will demo a live line configured for your business.

Do you work with dental, chiropractic, and medical practices under HIPAA?

Yes. We build HIPAA-aware: patient health information stays in your existing practice management, forms, and booking systems, never on the marketing site. Public review replies and social messaging follow strict zero-PHI rules, and we execute a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) when applicable.

Can I hire Daulx for just one service?

Yes. Start with a single piece — like fixing your AI search visibility, rebuilding an outdated website, or turning on 24/7 AI phone answering — and add more only when it makes sense.
