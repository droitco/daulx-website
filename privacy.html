Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Daulx, LLC

What we collect

When you request a lead-leak audit, contact us, or opt in to text messages on daulx.com, we collect the name, business name, mobile number, and email address you provide, along with any message details you submit.

How we use your information

We use your information to prepare and deliver your requested audit, respond to your inquiry, service your account, and send the scheduling or account text messages you explicitly requested.

Text messaging & mobile privacy

Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing any service. Message frequency varies, and standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for help, or email brandon@daulx.com.

No mobile opt-in data or text message consent will ever be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing & service providers

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with trusted infrastructure providers required to deliver our services (such as telecommunications carriers and email/messaging delivery platforms), strictly to perform services on behalf of Daulx, LLC.

Regulated healthcare clients (HIPAA)

This public marketing website does not collect Protected Health Information (PHI). When Daulx, LLC provides services to regulated medical, dental, or chiropractic practices, patient intake and health records remain inside the practice's designated HIPAA-compliant software systems under applicable Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).

Retention & your choices

  • Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Request a copy of or deletion of the contact information we hold about you by emailing brandon@daulx.com.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to Daulx, LLC at brandon@daulx.com.
